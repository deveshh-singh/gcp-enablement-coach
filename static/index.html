Find out what you really took in.

Give it a document, a web page or your own notes for a quiz on what it says. Give it a job posting and it researches the key skills, so you can practise for the role.

Paste a job posting to practise its key skills, or use an article or documentation. Pages behind a login can't be read.

This app doesn't save your document. It's sent to Gemini to write the questions, and the quiz is kept for up to an hour so it can be marked. For a job posting, its key skills are searched on the web. Please don't upload anything confidential.

    How it works

    1. Gemini reads your materialLinks are fetched by Gemini on Google Cloud, not by this app's server.
    2. A job posting gets extra researchGemini picks out the key skills and searches the web for each one.
    3. Gemini writes the questionsIt works only from your material or the research, in a fixed format.
    4. Code checks and marks everythingEvery question is validated, and answers stay on the server until you submit.
    An early version, with no accounts yet. A daily limit keeps the running cost under control.
