# AI Solutions Engineer, Example Logistics

Example Logistics is a made-up company, used here to show how the coach handles a job posting.

Our customers want to use generative AI on their own shipping documents and support
tickets. As an AI Solutions Engineer you will work directly with those customers to
understand the problem, build a working prototype and take it to production.

## What you'll do
- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) applications over customer documents.
- Evaluate LLM output for accuracy, and reduce made-up answers.
- Integrate models with customer systems through REST APIs.
- Deploy containerised services to a public cloud.
- Explain technical trade-offs to customer teams, including non-technical stakeholders.

## What we're looking for
- Strong Python and solid SQL.
- Hands-on experience with LLM APIs, embeddings and vector search.
- Experience with Docker and a public cloud platform.
- Clear written and spoken communication.

This is a full-time role with applications open now.
